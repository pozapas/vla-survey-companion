# Data license

The CSV files in `data/` are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). The full license text is at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The license covers the compilation, the selection of systems and results, and the coding of every row, including the class codes, the verification fields, and the location columns. It does not cover the short excerpts quoted from source papers in `claims.csv`, `claims_tables.csv`, `longhorizon_pairs.csv`, and `positioning_evidence.csv`. Those excerpts remain with their copyright holders and are included as brief quotations so that each number and table cell can be checked against its source.

When you reuse the data, please cite the survey and link to this repository.
